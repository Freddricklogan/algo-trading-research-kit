Walk-forward report

Data: $source, $days daily closes. Costs: $commission bps commission + $slippage bps slippage per unit of turnover. Every figure on this page was computed by algo-research report in the CI run that published it. The series is synthetic; nothing here is a forecast or a recommendation.

$sections

Method, stated

  • Signal on day t is traded at the close of day t+1; no look-ahead.
  • Cost per unit of turnover = (commission + slippage) bps ÷ 10,000, charged on every change of position.
  • Walk-forward: parameters chosen by in-sample Sharpe on a $train_days-day window, traded on the next $test_days days, rolled forward. Only out-of-sample days are concatenated.
  • Deflated Sharpe ratio after Bailey and López de Prado (2014): the probability that the best-of-grid Sharpe exceeds what selection among that many trials would produce by chance.
  • Sharpe annualised with √252 and a zero risk-free rate.
